# Code of Conduct

The project should remain a place where people can improve technical work together. Communicate with respect, focus feedback on the work and its impact, welcome different viewpoints and levels of experience, and revise a claim when the evidence changes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately through the repository host. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-managed communication channels.
