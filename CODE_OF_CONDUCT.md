# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with respect and professionalism;
- welcome different viewpoints and levels of experience;
- focus feedback on the work and its impact; and
- accept correction and revise claims when evidence changes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, and deliberate disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately through the repository host. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-managed communication channels.
